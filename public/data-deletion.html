Delete Your Data

Updated 17 November 2026

Disconnect a social account

Open PeakCut → Settings → Connected accounts and choose Disconnect next to the account. This removes the locally saved account connection and tokens. Revoke PeakCut separately in that platform’s Apps and Websites or connected-app settings if you also want to withdraw platform authorization.

Delete video projects

Use PeakCut’s project or full-workspace deletion controls to remove local videos and generated clips. Review any local backups or exported copies separately. Posts already published must be deleted on the destination platform.

Clear browser pairing

Disconnect the local engine or clear this site’s browser storage to remove the remembered pairing key. Removing the browser pairing alone does not delete videos or revoke social access.

Help

Use the project repository for general help. Never put private data or credentials in a public issue.
